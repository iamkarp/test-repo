# Karpeles Museum - SEM Automatic Marketer

An automated Search Engine Marketing (SEM) campaign generator specifically designed for Karpeles Manuscript Library Museums. This tool helps create, optimize, and manage SEM campaigns that promote the museum's free admission and educational value.

## Overview

The Karpeles Manuscript Library Museums house the world's largest private collection of original manuscripts and documents, with locations across the United States offering **completely free admission**. This SEM automation tool is designed to maximize visibility and visitor engagement while emphasizing the unique "free museum" value proposition.

## Optimization Tips

1. **Start Small**: Begin with brand and free admission campaigns
2. **Test Ad Copy**: Run A/B tests on "free admission" messaging
3. **Track Conversions**: Set up "Get Directions" clicks as conversions
4. **Adjust Bids**: Increase bids on weekends for family searches
5. **Use Extensions**: Add all relevant ad extensions for better CTR
6. **Monitor Quality Score**: Aim for 7+ on all keywords
7. **Negative Keywords**: Regularly update negative keyword lists
8. **Seasonal Campaigns**: Boost budgets during school field trip season

## About Karpeles Manuscript Library Museums

The Karpeles Manuscript Library Museums were established to promote the reading of important original documents of literary, musical, scientific, historical, and social interest. The museums display original manuscripts and documents, and admission is always free to the public.

### Mission
To provide the public with free access to important original manuscripts and documents, promoting literacy, education, and historical appreciation.

### Why Free Admission?
The Karpeles family believes that everyone should have access to history and culture, regardless of their ability to pay. This commitment to free admission makes SEM marketing particularly effective, as "free museum" is a highly searched and valued proposition.
